About Us

Ensuring Clean Water for a Safer Tomorrow

Our Story 💧

TRUE BLUE Water is life, yet many struggle to get clean and safe water. To solve this, we built a Smart Water Quality Monitoring System that tracks pH, turbidity, TDS, and temperature in real time. Our mission is to use IoT and data analytics to make water monitoring smarter, faster, and accessible for homes, industries, and communities.

Our Vision 🌍

We envision a future where every drop of water consumed is safe, clean, and healthy. By leveraging smart sensors, cloud-based data processing, and instant alerts, we aim to create a sustainable water management ecosystem.

Physical Test

Temperature

Color & Odor

Chlorine & Nitrate

TDS
